Print a cabinet handle template at the right scale

What to measurePrint settingsCheck the paperBefore marking

A printable cabinet handle template is a marking aid, not proof that the printed holes are the right distance apart. Start with your measured hole pitch, then check the paper with a physical ruler before transferring any marks.

The cabinet handle template generator makes a one-page SVG for one horizontal, two-hole drawer pull. It accepts millimetres or decimal inches and offers A4 or US Letter landscape. The example values are not a recommendation for your hardware or drawer.

Measure hole centres, not the handle's overall length

Hole pitch is the distance from the centre of one fixing hole to the centre of the other. Keep that separate from the full length of the grip. The top-edge offset is another measurement: in this tool it starts at the drawn top datum, not at the edge of the paper.

Choose the paper, then check the scaling controls

  1. Enter the pitch you measured and the offset you chose. Select the paper actually loaded in your printer.
  2. Generate and download the SVG. Keep its physical dimensions intact; pasting it into a document or taking a screenshot introduces another sizing step.
  3. In the application printing the file, look for 100% or an actual-size setting rather than an automatic fit option. Names and options vary by application and printer.

If you have separately converted the drawing to PDF, Adobe's PDF printing guide distinguishes Actual size from Fit: Actual size does not scale the page, while Fit can shrink or enlarge it. Cropping is possible at actual size. That documentation is for PDF, not an SVG-opening instruction; conversion can introduce its own scaling.

Measure both 50 mm calibration bars

The generated sheet includes a horizontal and a vertical 50 mm bar. Measure both on the printed paper, then measure the two marked hole centres and compare them with the actual handle. A screen preview is not a physical ruler.

If either bar is wrong, or the axes disagree, stop and investigate the print settings or print path. Do not assume changing a percentage will fix every cause. Reprint and remeasure after any change.

Before marking the drawer

This is not a drill guide. After checking the print, align the drawn datum and centreline to your own measured references. Mark the points, remove the paper, recheck, and test on scrap before working on the drawer.

The tool does not choose placement, safe edge distance, bit diameter, screw length, compatibility or load capacity. It does not cover a one-hole knob or a vertical handle. Physical print accuracy has not been verified for your printer.
